v2 -- intermediate states

Every meaningfully distinct visual state the archipelago-map stub passed through on its way to the version in ../v2/, each rebuilt with its own dependency chain as it stood at that commit.

  1. 01 -- initial (c384a11)

    The very first pass: hand-drawn hachure/islet flourishes, a plain double-rectangle cartouche, simple diamond compass needle. Built before the map was reviewed against its own reference images.

  2. 02 -- Round 1 visual pass (e8694a4)

    The reference-driven overhaul: jaggier engraved coastlines, correlated ripple contours, hachure ticks, an 8-point compass star, corner-scroll cartouche flourishes, topic-matched entry icons.

  3. 03 -- coastline regen (b20398f)

    Islands repositioned for a new layout; ripple rings switched from per-island triples to one fused distance-transform contour. Drops the old per-island hachure/islet flourishes -- they didn't fit the new coastline style.

  4. 04 -- serpent redesign (a3887b0)

    Same map as 03, with the sea-serpent motif replaced: a segmented coiling body with head, eye, frill and tail, instead of a bare sine-wave-plus-triangle.
