# Choices

*Jesal Mehta, 2023*

Miles to go before I sleep  
In snowy evening or rainy morn  
And roads keep diverging on and on  
In woods and fields,  
Yellow or green or brown  
A fractal branching of the what-if tree,  
Multiverses spinning out of each moment  
Everything everywhere and all that jazz  
But I am here, now,  
And the paths fade.  
Is adulthood  
simply  
the pruning of possibility?
