# Chopped Papaya

*Jesal Mehta, 2023*

*(I stood at the fridge door, as we do...)*

Chopped papaya pieces  
In a vessel  
in the fridge  
Two days old  
And sweeter still

Disappearing with every passage  
Past the kitchen door  
You wait for the prize at the bottom  
Where the juice has collected  
Pale orange, pearlescent  
The jewels of juice jelly.
