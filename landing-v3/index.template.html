Cabinet of Curiosities

A map of worlds, of explorations across them, of curiosities followed, and the rabbitholes at the end of the rainbow.

Cabinets of Curiosities were personal collections that predated modern museums as collections of interesting, beautiful, unusual or rare objects from around the world. This is my Cabinet of Curiosities, a collection of interesting bits from around my world. You are welcome to browse it. I hope it brings delight and, ofcourse, ignites curiosity.
