Cabinet Islands

A permanent showcase for the noise/domain-warp coastline generator (cabinet-v3-islandshape.js) built for the v3 layout prototype. Runs on a frozen snapshot of the real site's content -- same titles/weights, no live links. Tune the shapes live with the panel; "Copy config" feeds discoveries back into cabinet-v3-data.js. See Landing-page-notes.2.0.md for the design conversation behind this.
